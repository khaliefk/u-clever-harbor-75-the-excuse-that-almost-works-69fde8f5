A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5) bounty entry. The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: the text a gamer sends the boss to skip work during GTA 6 launch week.

The excuse is deliberately boring. A council water shutoff is the kind of thing a tired manager has heard before and can almost believe, and the punchline sits in the excuse itself: you cannot turn up unwashed. No drama, no dead relatives, no flat tyre, just a flat where the taps ran dry until Thursday.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```

The grader runs `main.py` in a sandbox with a fresh `/output` directory and reads `/output/entry.txt`. The entry is one line, 18 words, 95 characters, plain UTF-8 text.

## The line

> Sorry boss, the council cut our water off till Thursday and I can't come in unwashed like this.
